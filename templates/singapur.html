SINGAPORE

Singapore offers budget travelers an exciting blend of cultural diversity, modernity, and convenience. Accommodations here are known for their cleanliness, friendly atmosphere, and social vibe, making it easy to connect with fellow explorers. From your base, you can effortlessly visit iconic landmarks like Marina Bay Sands, Gardens by the Bay, and Chinatown thanks to the efficient public transportation system. Additionally, savoring affordable and delicious street food at bustling hawker centers is a highlight. Whether you're drawn to the sights, local flavors, or the vibrant nightlife, Singapore provides a welcoming and communal environment for your adventures.

ITINERARY

How to spend a day in Singapore

- - - - ( 7:00 AM ) - - - -
Breakfast at the Hostel Start your day with a hearty breakfast at your hostel. Many hostels offer complimentary breakfast, so take advantage of it to fuel up for the day ahead.
- - - - ( 8:00 AM ) - - - -
Visit Gardens by the BayHead to Gardens by the Bay, a beautiful park with iconic Supertree structures and themed gardens. You can explore the Cloud Forest, Flower Dome, and take a walk around the outdoor gardens.
- - - - ( 10:00 AM ) - - - -
Explore Marina Bay Sands Make your way to Marina Bay Sands, one of Singapore's most famous landmarks. You can visit the ArtScience Museum, take a stroll along the Marina Bay Sands Promenade, or simply enjoy the stunning views of the city skyline.
- - - - ( 1:00 PM ) - - - -
LunchFor lunch, visit a nearby hawker center to experience authentic Singaporean street food. Try local favorites like chicken rice, laksa, or satay. Some popular hawker centers include Lau Pa Sat and Newton Food Centre.
- - - - ( 2:30 PM ) - - - -
Visit Chinatown Head to Chinatown to explore the vibrant streets, traditional shops, and historical landmarks. Don't miss the Buddha Tooth Relic Temple, Thian Hock Keng Temple, and the colorful shophouses.
- - - - ( 4:00 PM ) - - - -
Walk around Clarke Quay Take a leisurely walk along Clarke Quay, a riverside entertainment hub with plenty of bars, restaurants, and shops. You can enjoy the scenic views of the Singapore River and maybe even catch a boat ride.
- - - - ( 6:00 PM ) - - - -
Dinner For dinner, choose a local restaurant that offers a variety of cuisines. You can try dishes like Hainanese chicken rice, chili crab, or dim sum. Some popular dining spots include Maxwell Food Centre and Lau Pa Sat.
- - - - ( 9:00 PM ) - - - -
Night SafariEnd your day with a visit to the Night Safari, where you can see nocturnal animals in their natural habitats, or head to the Marina Bay Sands Observation Deck for a stunning nighttime view of the city.
