MANALI

Manali, nestled in the Himalayas, is a serene destination for hostel travelers seeking adventure and natural beauty. Hostels in Manali offer affordable stays with stunning views, creating a social atmosphere where travelers can connect and share experiences. Enjoy trekking, paragliding, and exploring quaint villages, all while staying in a friendly and communal environment that enhances the charm of this picturesque hill station.

ITINERARY

How to spend a day in Manali

- - - - ( 7:00 AM ) - - - -
Breakfast at the Hostel Start your day with a hearty breakfast at your hostel. Many hostels offer a variety of options to kickstart your day.
- - - - ( 8:00 AM ) - - - -
Visit Hadimba TempleHead to this ancient temple early to avoid the crowds. It's a great way to experience the spiritual vibes and panoramic views.
- - - - ( 10:00 AM ) - - - -
Explore Old ManaliWander through the charming streets of Old Manali, enjoy local shopping, and soak in the vibrant atmosphere.
- - - - ( 1:00 PM ) - - - -
Lunch Try some authentic Himachali cuisine at a nearby restaurant. Look for places that offer thali meals for a variety of dishes.
- - - - ( 2:30 PM ) - - - -
Visit Vashisht Baths Relax in the hot springs and rejuvenate yourself. It's a perfect spot to unwind and enjoy the natural beauty.
- - - - ( 4:00 PM ) - - - -
Relax at Jogini Falls Take a leisurely walk to Jogini Falls and enjoy the scenic beauty. It's a peaceful spot to unwind and take in the views.
- - - - ( 6:00 PM ) - - - -
Shopping at Mall RoadWander through the bustling market and pick up some souvenirs, local crafts, or traditional textiles.
- - - - ( 9:00 PM ) - - - -
Dinner End your day with a delicious dinner. Many hostels have communal kitchens or dining areas where you can socialize with fellow travelers.
