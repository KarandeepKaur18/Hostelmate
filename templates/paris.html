PARIS

Paris, the City of Light, is a dream destination for hostel travelers offering a blend of history, culture, and romance on a budget. Hostels in Paris provide affordable accommodations close to iconic landmarks like the Eiffel Tower, Louvre Museum, and Notre-Dame Cathedral, creating a social atmosphere where travelers can connect and share experiences. Wander through charming neighborhoods, savor delicious pastries, and soak in the artistic ambiance, all while staying in a friendly and communal hostel environment.

ITINERARY

How to spend a day in Paris

- - - - ( 7:00 AM ) - - - -
Breakfast at the Hostel Start your day with a hearty breakfast at your hostel. Many hostels offer a variety of options to kickstart your day.
- - - - ( 8:00 AM ) - - - -
Visit the Eiffel TowerHead to the iconic Eiffel Tower early to avoid the crowds. Enjoy the stunning views of Paris from the top.
- - - - ( 10:00 AM ) - - - -
Stroll along the Seine River Take a leisurely walk along the Seine River and enjoy the beautiful scenery. You can also visit the famous bookstalls along the riverbanks.
- - - - ( 1:00 PM ) - - - -
Lunch Try some authentic French cuisine at a nearby café. Look for places that offer classic dishes like croissants, baguettes, and café au lait.
- - - - ( 2:30 PM ) - - - -
Visit the Louvre Museum Explore the world-renowned Louvre Museum and marvel at masterpieces like the Mona Lisa and the Venus de Milo.
- - - - ( 4:00 PM ) - - - -
Relax at the Luxembourg Gardens Take a break at the beautiful Luxembourg Gardens, a peaceful oasis in the heart of the city. Enjoy the well-manicured gardens and charming pathways.
- - - - ( 6:00 PM ) - - - -
Explore Montmartre Wander through the artistic streets of Montmartre, known for its bohemian charm and vibrant atmosphere. Visit the famous Sacré-Cœur Basilica and enjoy the panoramic views of Paris.
- - - - ( 9:00 PM ) - - - -
Dinner End your day with a delicious dinner at a local restaurant. Many hostels have communal kitchens or dining areas where you can socialize with fellow travelers.
