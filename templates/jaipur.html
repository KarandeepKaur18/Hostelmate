JAIPUR

Jaipur, known as the Pink City, is a vibrant destination for hostel travelers offering an affordable and culturally rich experience. Hostels in Jaipur are budget-friendly and located close to historic landmarks such as the Amber Fort, City Palace, and Hawa Mahal, fostering a social environment where travelers can connect and share their adventures. Enjoy exploring bustling markets, savoring local cuisine, and immersing yourself in the city's royal heritage, all while staying in a welcoming and friendly hostel atmosphere.

ITINERARY

How to spend a day in Jaipur

- - - - ( 7:00 AM ) - - - -
Breakfast at the Hostel Start your day with a hearty breakfast at your hostel. Many hostels offer a variety of options to kickstart your day.
- - - - ( 8:00 AM ) - - - -
Visit Hawa MahalHead to the iconic "Palace of Winds" early to avoid the crowds. It's a great way to get a stunning view of Jaipur's architecture.
- - - - ( 10:00 AM ) - - - -
Explore Jantar MantarJust a short walk from Hawa Mahal, this UNESCO World Heritage site is an astronomical observatory with fascinating instruments.
- - - - ( 1:00 PM ) - - - -
Lunch Try some authentic Rajasthani cuisine at a nearby restaurant. Look for places that offer thali meals for a variety of dishes.
- - - - ( 2:30 PM ) - - - -
Visit City Palace and Chandra Mahal Explore the grandeur of Jaipur's royal history. The palace complex offers beautiful gardens and stunning views.
- - - - ( 4:00 PM ) - - - -
Relax at Bani Park Take a leisurely stroll or relax at a nearby park in the Bani Park area. It's a peaceful spot to unwind.
- - - - ( 6:00 PM ) - - - -
Shopping at Johari Bazaar Wander through the bustling markets and pick up some souvenirs, jewelry, or traditional textiles.
- - - - ( 9:00 PM ) - - - -
Dinner End your day with a delicious dinner. Many hostels have communal kitchens or dining areas where you can socialize with fellow travelers.
