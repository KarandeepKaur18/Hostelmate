GOA

Goa, a paradise for hostel travelers, offers a perfect mix of sun, sand, and vibrant nightlife, making it an ideal destination for budget-conscious adventurers. Hostels in Goa provide affordable accommodations close to stunning beaches, lively markets, and diverse dining options, fostering a social atmosphere where travelers can easily connect and share experiences. Enjoy water sports, beach parties, and the rich cultural heritage, all while staying in a friendly and communal environment.

ITINERARY

How to spend a day in Goa

- - - - ( 7:00 AM ) - - - -
Breakfast at the Hostel Start your day with a delicious breakfast at your hostel. Many hostels in Goa offer a variety of local and international dishes to kickstart your day.
- - - - ( 8:00 AM ) - - - -
Morning Beach Yoga SessionHead to the beach for a refreshing morning yoga session. Many hostels offer yoga classes on the beach, providing a perfect start to your day with some relaxation and mindfulness.
- - - - ( 10:00 AM ) - - - -
Explore Old Goa and Basilica of Bom Jesus Visit Old Goa to explore its historic churches and landmarks. Don't miss the Basilica of Bom Jesus, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and the Se Cathedral.
- - - - ( 1:00 PM ) - - - -
LunchFor lunch, head to a nearby beach shack to enjoy fresh seafood and local Goan cuisine. Beach shacks are a great way to experience the local food culture.
- - - - ( 2:30 PM ) - - - -
Relax at Anjuna Beach Take a break and relax at Anjuna Beach. You can swim, sunbathe, or simply enjoy the serene atmosphere. It's a great spot to unwind and soak in the beauty of Goa.
- - - - ( 4:00 PM ) - - - -
- Explore Arambol Beach Head to Arambol Beach for some more beach time. You can try water sports like paddleboarding or simply take a leisurely walk along the shore.
- - - - ( 6:00 PM ) - - - -
Dinner at a Local Restaurant For dinner, choose a local restaurant that offers a variety of Goan dishes. Enjoy a meal with fresh ingredients and authentic flavors.
- - - - ( 9:00 PM ) - - - -
Nightlife at a Beach Club End your day with some nightlife at a beach club. Enjoy music, dancing, and drinks while soaking in the vibrant atmosphere of Goa's nightlife.
