RISHIKESH

Rishikesh, known as the "Yoga Capital of the World," is a serene destination for hostel travelers seeking spirituality and adventure. Hostels in Rishikesh offer affordable stays close to the Ganges River, ashrams, and yoga centers, fostering a social atmosphere where travelers can connect and share their spiritual journeys. Enjoy activities like white-water rafting, meditation, and exploring the scenic beauty of the Himalayas, all while staying in a friendly and communal environment.

ITINERARY

How to spend a day in Rishikesh

- - - - ( 7:00 AM ) - - - -
Breakfast at the Hostel Start your day with a hearty breakfast at your hostel. Many hostels offer a variety of options to kickstart your day.
- - - - ( 8:00 AM ) - - - -
Visit Lakshman JhulaHead to this iconic suspension bridge early to avoid the crowds. It's a great way to experience the spiritual vibes and enjoy the scenic views of the Ganges River.
- - - - ( 10:00 AM ) - - - -
Explore Tera Manzil MandirJust a short walk from Lakshman Jhula, this 13-floor temple offers panoramic views of the river and the surrounding hills.
- - - - ( 1:00 PM ) - - - -
LunchTry some authentic Indian cuisine at a nearby restaurant. Look for places that offer thali meals for a variety of dishes.
- - - - ( 2:30 PM ) - - - -
Visit Triveni Ghat Attend the Ganga Aarti at Triveni Ghat, a spiritual experience where you can witness the evening prayers and rituals.
- - - - ( 4:00 PM ) - - - -
Relax at Neer Garh Waterfall Take a leisurely walk to Neer Garh Waterfall and enjoy the serene beauty of the cascading water. It's a peaceful spot to unwind.
- - - - ( 6:00 PM ) - - - -
Shopping at local markets Wander through the bustling markets and pick up some souvenirs, local crafts, or traditional textiles.
- - - - ( 9:00 PM ) - - - -
Dinner End your day with a delicious dinner. Many hostels have communal kitchens or dining areas where you can socialize with fellow travelers.
